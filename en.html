How a Meta campaign really creates customers

More than three billion people use Meta's apps every day. Advertising there works like an auction: for every impression, Meta picks the ad most likely to deliver the requested result. My job is to make your ads win, at the best cost.

Objective

Sales, leads, WhatsApp conversations or awareness: the algorithm optimises for what you ask.

Audience

Broad targeting, interests, custom and lookalike audiences, locations and languages.

Creatives

Several angles and formats tested in parallel: today the creative is the main targeting lever.

Bidding and budget

Campaign structure, budget allocation, pacing and the learning phase.

Measurement

Pixel, Conversions API and conversation tracking to know what really sells.

Optimisation

Cut what costs, scale what converts, refresh creatives before fatigue sets in.

The metrics I manage for you

Cost per mille

My creative method

On Meta, the creative now does most of the targeting. One video is a gamble. Ten well-built videos are a method.

Frequently asked questions

What ad budget should I plan?

It depends on your market and goals. We set it together during the audit. Ad spend is always paid directly to Meta from your account, on top of my fees.

How fast will I see results?

First data comes in during week one. It usually takes two to four weeks of testing to find the creatives and audiences that perform, then we scale.

Do you work with companies outside Africa?

Yes. I work remotely, in French, with companies targeting West Africa, Europe or both.

Who keeps control of the ad account?

You do. I work inside your Ads Manager with partner access. You see everything, at any time.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Your numbers, audiences and creatives are never shared. A confidentiality agreement can be signed before we start.

How does payment work?

By international bank transfer, card or mobile money, with an official invoice from Extraxium Digital SARL.
